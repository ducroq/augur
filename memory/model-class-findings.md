# Model-class findings — standing conclusions

Moved out of `memory/MEMORY.md` on 2026-09-11 when the index passed its 50k ceiling. This is the judgement layer for model work: read it before proposing any, alongside the *Decision state* section of `docs/experiment-results.md` (generated from the registry, so it cannot drift).

⚠️ **Read this correction first — it re-scales every number below.** EXP-036 (2026-09-11) replaced the single-day carry these conclusions are measured against with an honest profile floor, and the carry turned out to be **15.8% too weak**. Consequences:

- **The foundation model's headline halves.** Chronos-bolt-base is **+24.8%** over the carry but **+10.7%** over the profile floor — **57%** of the margin was ordinary profile smoothing that a seven-line baseline also has. Anywhere below says −20.3% or +25.0%, quote this beside it. The *direction* is unchanged: Chronos still clears every arm in every month, on MAE and on cost regret.
- **The incumbent is further under water than stated.** LightGBM `full` goes from +5.1% over the carry to **−12.4%** below the profile floor, and from 2 of 9 months below to **7 of 9**.
- **A scalar floor is the wrong shape.** The profile's advantage is horizon-dependent (+7.1% at 1–24h, +19.8% at 49–72h). Successor criteria are per horizon group.
- **The product lens is harsher, not kinder** (EXP-037): the incumbent ranks cheap hours *worse* than the carry — regret 7.99 vs 5.01 EUR/MWh, worse in 6 of 9 months. Mechanism is **amplitude collapse**: beyond 24h it forecasts ~⅓ of the true diurnal spread, which improves MAE and destroys ranking. The training objective is buying MAE by destroying the product value.

## Standing conclusions after EXP-021..031 (as written 2026-08-30)

**Settled, do not re-litigate** (each closed by a test that could have refuted it; ADR-007 forbids re-running a refuted position with a looser Method — a new bet needs a new Position and a mechanism explaining why the earlier test could not have seen the effect):
- **Feature engineering is closed for LightGBM at 56 days.** Adding exogenous columns buys nothing (EXP-018/020); adding *level* columns actively costs — confirmed four independent ways: internal rolling mean (EXP-019), exogenous gas (EXP-020), residual-regression level columns (EXP-029), and matched-count derived columns (EXP-024).
- **The FM's context advantage is not recoverable inside LightGBM.** Widening the 14-number feature row fails (EXP-024, +2.5% worse). The bottleneck is model class, not feature-row width.
- **Do not fine-tune Chronos on this asset.** Both skill and calibration degrade (EXP-027: MAE +19%, coverage 0.825→0.394). Only ~3.6k pre-cutoff points exist. Zero-shot is the deployment mode. *Caveat: naive recipe, no early stopping — a careful recipe was never tried.*
- **Model size is not a constraint, and neither is latency.** tiny retains 93.5% (EXP-026); base runs a 72h forecast in 0.52s on 4 threads (EXP-030). Deployment cost is the torch dependency, not compute.

**Live decisions, in order of cheapness** (revisit triggers + review-by dates in `docs/hypothesis-log.md`):
1. **Chronos-2 covariates** (EXP-028, `parked`) — +8.3% QS, Augur's first positive exogenous result, **but it fails the coverage gate** and contamination is not excluded by construction (weights modified 2026-06-05, inside the window). Confirmation pre-committed as **EXP-028a** (≥28 fresh vintages ≈2026-09-23), which is also the only thing that resolves the contamination exposure by construction.
2. **112-day window** (EXP-023, `parked`) — **demoted 2026-08-30**: EXP-023a Stage A (EXP-032) found the advantage does *not* reproduce on 56 unscored vintages — 112d is **1.4% worse**, DM p=0.894, sign flipped from the discovery's −3.0%. Alternative 1 (regime artifact) fires: 112d wins in the May–Aug rising-price stretch, loses in calmer Mar–May. Stage A is a *quasi*-holdout so this does not formally refute EXP-023, but it is no longer a cheap near-certain win. Stage B (≥45 fresh vintages ≈2026-10-09) is now the real test.
3. **EXP-021a** — the confirmation ladder for the foundation model: fresh vintages → feasibility → live shadow → only then an ADR-006 amendment.

**Numbers not to requote:** the FM's calibration edge is **0.811 vs lean+CQR 0.788**, *not* vs the pre-conformal 0.611 (EXP-025). And EXP-021's stated mechanism (level drift) was **wrong** — it is a pretrained distributional prior plus a feature-row information bottleneck (EXP-022).
